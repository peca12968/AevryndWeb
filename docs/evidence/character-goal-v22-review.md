# Character goal v22 review

2026-10-20. User request: redesign the continuous-goal prompt. This is prompt maintenance only; no model production, adoption, scoring or stagnation-counter changes.

## Why the execution contract changed

The previous contract allowed scoped functional repair to keep displacing the visibly unfinished face and hair. r74 added complete-source oral controls; r75 repaired opposing arches within a limited rigid-motion scope; the interrupted r76 job produced a neighboring-crown candidate. These are distinct, potentially useful results, but do not prove that the complete woman looks realistic or that the entire mouth, hair, clothing or engine is accepted.

v22 keeps the full objective and acceptance standard, and adds enforceable scheduling: while complete appearance is not credible, at least two of each three actual production rounds directly author a major visible defect. Invisible dependencies/support diagnosis are limited to two consecutive rounds. Historical work is classified by what was actually done, without relabeling or resetting counters. Preserve unfinished dependency layers as explicitly unaccepted, use safe rollback or a different dependency design, and return to a complete-character deliverable. Do not hide or waive contact failures.

## Review against likely failure modes

1. Another isolated tooth proof cannot reset whole-character stagnation or be reported as face/hair realism. It may be retained only within its actual scope.
2. A cheap preview that disproves the authoring idea stops that method before costly unrelated verification.
3. An actual hair/face improvement needs complete-head/body A/B, relevant second angle/light and affected checks; pixel difference or a reference image is insufficient.
4. After dependency limits, preserve failures rather than hide them, and change the work toward a complete-character result. Existing historical counters remain intact.
5. Missing contact, motion, rights or runtime gates still prevent formal promotion, a complete score and male-character work. The 85 overall / 80 each gates and explicit female user approval remain.

These are contract checks, not observed proof that future production will obey or improve. Behavioral effectiveness remains unvalidated until actual later results.
