# Female character r0507

This Blender file contains two unaccepted posterior hair layouts on the r0500 B woman and the r0505 B comparison. Open at frame 1. R507 complete hair | overlapping is visible. Use the Outliner to select one complete groom at a time for comparison. R507 complete hair | separated is A and R505 full hair | layered is the control. Frame 37 shows the existing 32-degree held head turn.

Each r0507 layout rebuilds 10,416 posterior curves around 13 original guide paths. Seven upper paths and six lower paths define the new sweep and tip release. A gives each curve one guide corridor. B blends adjacent corridors at their edges and staggers the longer underlayer. The native curve-domain attribute AEVRYND_R507_LAYER maps 0 to upper, 1 to lower, and -1 to retained source curves. AEVRYND_R507_LOCK identifies the corridor within each layer. The first eight control points on each curve are pinned to the source, with original follicle IDs, radii, materials and head binding.

A shows a conspicuous opening in the rear and pronounced ridges during the held turn. B closes the central opening and shows a clearer diagonal posterior path and staggered ends. A dark outer join and broad crown highlights still limit the full hairstyle. The rear close-up is framed to compare lock boundaries. The rear three-quarter and held-turn views show the complete hair outline.

The stored guide centre objects document the new routes. The authoring script regenerates the native curves from measured r0505 trajectories. The .blend contains the complete source, including the actual editable render curves. The sidecar guide-centres.npz records the guide positions and curve ownership used in verification.

The contact receipt covers native finite-radius curves and consecutive sampled chords of the changed population against the actual posed skin at the listed frames. It records every inspected mode, signed clearances and witness positions. Continuous-time, strand-to-strand and garment contact and Aevrynd engine playback require separate evidence.

The guide workflow reference is the [Blender Hair Nodes manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/geometry_nodes/hair/index.html). The r0507 curves and guide locations were constructed in Aevrynd from the inherited source. Attribution and rights are in licenses.
