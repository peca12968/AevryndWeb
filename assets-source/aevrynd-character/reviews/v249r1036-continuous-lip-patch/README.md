# v249 r1036 continuous lip patch review

r1036 tests whether a separate Aevrynd-owned, three-ring lip annulus can conceal the collision-safe r1035 mouth aperture while preserving a narrow neutral slit and matching mouth-open and smile shape keys.

- All three variants are rejected. Neutral body-to-patch overlap is 1,130–1,185 triangle pairs, and both expression audits fail.
- The oral layers themselves remain clear of the patch, confirming that the failure is at the face-to-lip interface.
- The fixed-light renders show a visible oval perimeter, floating wetline fragments and a mask-like surface discontinuity. This is structurally and visually unacceptable for a human mouth.
- Skin material, scene lights, exposure and color management are unchanged. Website v83 remains 5.56% darker in embedded skin base color than the committed Blender mainline.
- No blend candidate is saved, and website promotion remains forbidden. r1033 stays the facial mainline; r1035 stays a topology and collision proof only.

The next mouth study must rebuild the lip annulus inside the body mesh and share a welded boundary with the surrounding face. A detached overlay is no longer an accepted direction.
