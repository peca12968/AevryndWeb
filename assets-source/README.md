# Aevrynd source assets

This directory contains authoring sources that are never loaded by the runtime.
Every imported source must have an explicit version, origin, license, and digest
before it may be used to create an engine asset.

## Blender Human Base Meshes 1.4.1

- Origin: https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#assets
- Download: https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip
- Version date: 2026-01-20
- Archive SHA-256: `811F43ACCBB31A88266D932F8F5563B2D13586FCA0BA2693AAD1F5FE582B3515`
- License: CC0 1.0 / public domain dedication
- Selected source asset: `Body Female - Realistic`

The official bundle describes this asset as a realistic scan-data model with
multiresolution details. Aevrynd will substantially modify, retopologize, rig,
texture, and optimize it before runtime use. The selected female source is kept
as `aevrynd-character/aevrynd-female-cc0-base-v1.blend`; the complete upstream
bundle can be reproduced from the recorded URL and digest when required.

## Aevrynd female authoring stages

- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v2.blend`: separate sclera, iris, pupil, cornea,
  and limbal-ring layers for both eyes.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v3.blend`: Aevrynd facial-form pass plus
  surface-anchored brows and upper/lower lashes.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v4.blend`: short layered groom with separate scalp,
  primary guides, detail cards, hairline/nape hairs, and flyaways. The groom is
  generated by `scripts/build-female-hair-v4.py`; it imports no third-party hair
  asset or runtime dependency.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v5.blend`: calibrated iris scale plus separate tear
  meniscus and mouth-crease layers, generated entirely from Aevrynd geometry.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v6.blend`: Aevrynd-generated skin and sclera color
  maps connected to exportable glTF materials. Experimental normal and roughness
  maps are retained as source data but deliberately disconnected until tangent
  and UV-seam validation passes.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v7.blend`: bounded eye-aperture refinement with
  lashes and tear layers moved with their eyelids. Attachment distances are
  measured by `scripts/audit-female-attachments.py` against the evaluated body
  and parent hair surfaces.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v8.blend`: first Aevrynd-owned humanoid skeleton,
  four-influence body skinning, rigid head-layer parenting, and an embedded
  neutral/raised-arm deformation action. It uses no Rigify or runtime authoring
  dependency; `scripts/audit-female-rig.py` verifies attachment stability in
  both animation frames.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v9.blend`: first explicit skinned inner-clothing
  layers. The top and full-crotch shorts inherit the four-influence skeleton,
  carry authored thickness and clearance metadata, and are checked at neutral
  and raised-arm frames with exact body/garment triangle-overlap tests. This is
  still a construction layer: garment edge flow and tailoring remain below the
  visual review threshold.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v10.blend`: head-detail experiment with smaller
  pupils, a second eyelid refinement, separately skinned upper/lower lip layers,
  an embedded Aevrynd-generated hair-opacity texture, and 1,288 child fibers.
  `scripts/audit-female-head-v10.py` verifies zero body intersections for the
  scalp, primary hair, detail fibers and lips at neutral and turned-head frames.
  The groom silhouette and back-scalp appearance still fail visual review.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v11.blend`: full groom-topology replacement using
  tapered three-sided fibers following regional scalp flow. Primary hair,
  authoring guides, frontal/nape/sideburn transition hairs and flyaways are
  explicit layers. The scalp coat reflection that produced the circular back
  patch is removed. `scripts/audit-female-groom-v11.py` checks all groom layers
  against the body at neutral and turned-head frames.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v12.blend`: bounded facial secondary forms for the
  cheeks, tear troughs, philtrum, nasolabial region and chin, plus exportable
  regional skin tone, separate upper-eyelid folds and eye catchlights. The
  upper-lip clearance is recalibrated after the facial change.
  `scripts/audit-female-face-v12.py` checks the new layers at neutral and posed
  frames, while the inherited groom and clothing audits continue to enforce
  zero exact body/asset triangle intersections.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v13.blend`: asymmetrical body-conforming scalp
  hairline plus an explicit 57/136/1,756 large, medium and fine groom hierarchy.
  Front, nape and sideburn transition fibers are rebuilt around the new contour.
  `scripts/audit-female-groom-v13.py` uses the v13 layer layouts to verify every
  groom layer, scalp and lip surface against the body in neutral and posed
  frames. The side and rear haircut transitions remain below visual review
  quality and are recorded as blocking failures in the neighboring score file.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v14.blend`: lower-density hairline geometry plus
  separate temple and nape transition layers. Its build produces a complete
  7.80 MiB authoring preview and a 5.92 MiB LOD1 preview that omits the primary,
  hairline and flyaway layers while retaining the scalp, large and medium
  clumps, transition clumps, face layers, clothing and rig. The paired runtime
  test verifies a 24.1% byte reduction without losing skin color or deformation.
  `scripts/audit-female-groom-v14.py` checks all seven full groom layers against
  the body in both validation poses.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v15.blend`: rebuilt upper and lower lips with five
  surface rows, explicit cupid's bow and lower-lip volume, a sculpted mouth
  foundation, and an independently skinned wet-line layer. The former flat
  mouth-crease strip is removed. `scripts/audit-female-mouth-v15.py` verifies
  skin weights and zero body or upper/lower-lip triangle intersections in both
  validation poses. A true opening oral cavity and expression shapes remain
  required before this mouth can pass review.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v16.blend`: replaces the overlong forehead hairline
  fibers with 84 body-conforming transition ribbons measuring 3.1–8.1 mm and
  retunes the dark, warm and scalp hair materials so the groom separates under
  neutral lighting. `scripts/audit-female-hairline-v16.py` verifies zero exact
  body intersections for every hair layer at neutral and raised-arm frames.
  The full runtime preview is 7.56 MiB; LOD1 is 5.95 MiB and 21.3% smaller.
  A trial jaw/oral layer was rejected because this base lacks a true mouth-slit
  topology, so it is not included in this stable authoring stage.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v17.blend`: replaces the invalid all-black skin
  normal and roughness placeholders with deterministic 256 x 256 tile-edge
  continuous microstructure maps and connects both maps to the Blender and GLB
  skin material. The full runtime preview remains below the established 8 MiB
  gate at 7.73 MiB; LOD1 is 6.13 MiB and 20.8% smaller. The strict score stays
  at 72 because the subtle material correction does not yet resolve the face's
  broad planar forms or add region-specific pores, oiliness and fine anatomy.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v18.blend`: adds bounded sub-millimeter facial
  asymmetry across the cheek, tear-trough, nasolabial, nose and chin regions,
  plus a weighted volume-preserving smoothing pass over 1,123 planar face
  vertices. Neutral and posed audits remain finite and preserve zero exact
  hair, mouth and garment/body triangle intersections. The runtime previews
  remain 7.73 MiB and 6.13 MiB. The strict score stays at 72 because the
  multi-angle visual change is too subtle and the broad cheek and lower-face
  planes still need manual high-resolution sculpting and retopology.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v19.blend`: replaces the sub-millimeter face pass
  with a guarded 4.48 mm primary-form sculpt over 3,027 control vertices. It
  narrows the mandible and separates the zygomatic, buccal, chin and mental-
  crease forms while protecting the eye and mouth attachment zones. All
  inherited neutral and posed intersection audits remain at zero exact
  triangle overlaps, and the runtime previews remain 7.73 MiB and 6.13 MiB.
  The score is recalibrated to a strict visual-first 47/100: the visible face
  improvement does not compensate for the doll-like eyes, layered mouth,
  plastic skin, sparse groom, construction clothing and limited deformation.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v20.blend`: narrows the round eye apertures with a
  guarded orbital sculpt, enables the existing transparent corneas in review
  renders, shrinks the artificial catchlights and adds 52 bounded radial iris
  fibers per eye. Eyelashes and tear menisci are reprojected to a measured
  0.32 mm facial clearance; the dedicated eye audit verifies zero exact body
  intersections for folds, lashes, tear layers and iris fibers in both poses.
  Full and LOD1 previews remain under the size gates at 7.75 MiB and 6.14 MiB.
  The strict visual-first score rises only to 48/100 because the integrated lid
  anatomy, lash rhythm, skin, mouth, groom, clothing and deformation still fail.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v21.blend`: replaces the disconnected high-density
  scalp with a continuous 2,497-vertex surface projected from the character's
  own evaluated head at 1.8 mm clearance. Fifty-nine separately layered,
  translucent boundary clumps add side and nape breakup. Neutral and posed
  audits report zero exact body intersections for both new layers. The simpler
  foundation also reduces the full preview to 7.00 MiB and LOD1 to 5.39 MiB,
  a 23.0% reduction. Its strict score is 49/100: crown and front coverage are
  better, while the side/rear undercut silhouette and straight-fiber groom still
  require a full large/medium clump-card pass.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v25.blend`: adds 271 restrained regional skin-tone
  values across the body, with warm cheek/nose/chin zones and cooler under-eye
  variation, and correctly chains pore-scale bump detail after the existing
  exportable normal map. Roughness breakup is layered over the runtime map and
  the coat response is reduced. All 39 mesh objects plus the 21-bone rig and
  animation match the collision-approved v21 geometry exactly; inherited eye
  and hair audits remain at zero triangle overlaps in both test poses. Full and
  LOD1 previews remain 7.00 MiB and 5.39 MiB. Its strict score is 50/100 because
  the visible material gain is modest and the face forms, groom, clothing and
  deformation still remain below review quality. Hair-card, broad-ribbon and
  fiber-veil experiments v22-v24 were visually rejected and are not stable
  authoring stages.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v30.blend`: rebuilds the visible eye and mouth
  proportions on the collision-approved v25 base. The eye apertures are less
  round, the dark uniform eyelid-fold strips are replaced by tapered skin-tone
  folds, and guarded sub-1.6 mm form changes define the brow, lower lid, tear
  trough, nose base, philtrum, nasolabial transition, mouth corners and chin.
  The upper and lower lips are regenerated on the evaluated face with tapered
  depth instead of the former extra 3.0 mm upper-lip offset. Exact audits at
  neutral and posed frames report zero body intersections for every eye, mouth,
  hair and clothing layer; 26 of 39 mesh objects and the 21-bone rig remain
  unchanged. The full and LOD1 previews are 7.00 MiB and 5.40 MiB, a 22.9%
  reduction. Its strict score is 53/100: the facial proportions improve, while
  true oral topology, high-resolution lids, hair, clothing and corrective
  deformation still block review. v27-v29 were rejected for negligible visual
  change, plastic lip response, or measured face-layer intersections.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v33.blend`: adds an explicit 1,080-vertex skinned
  undercut-density surface beneath the existing groom. It is projected from
  the character's own evaluated head at 0.72 mm clearance and carries an
  exportable vertex-alpha fade from 0 to 0.58, so the side and nape boundaries
  dissolve into short root density instead of exposing a bald band. Neutral
  and posed audits report zero intersections with the body and with all nine
  existing hair layers. Runtime export preserves the alpha gradient in BLEND
  mode; full and LOD1 previews are 7.07 MiB and 5.46 MiB, a 22.7% reduction.
  Its strict score is 54/100 because the cleaner undercut does not yet fix the
  uniformly combed crown or supply convincing large and medium clumps. Opaque
  jagged and hard-edged undercut experiments v31-v32 were rejected.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v35.blend`: replaces the opaque scalp-cap edge with
  an exportable vertex-density transition over 13.5 mm. All 40 v33 mesh objects
  retain identical vertex positions and polygon topology, so the prior
  collision result remains intact; a fresh neutral/posed audit also reports
  zero body intersections for all ten hair layers. The GLB keeps the scalp
  alpha in BLEND mode and retains 21 bones and one animation. Full and LOD1
  previews are 7.09 MiB and 5.48 MiB, a 22.7% reduction. Its strict score is
  55/100: the forehead, temple and nape transitions are less helmet-like, but
  the primary groom still needs authored large/medium clumps and crown breakup.
  v34 was rejected because its wide cards produced saw-toothed ear and rear
  silhouettes.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v36.blend`: reshapes the female lower-third facial
  silhouette with a guarded 3.0 mm maximum jaw taper and sub-millimetre chin,
  malar, buccal, alar and nose-tip transitions. Exactly 1,875 body vertices
  change inside the recorded lower-face region; the other 39 mesh objects,
  21-bone rig and animation remain unchanged. Fresh neutral and posed audits
  report zero body intersections for the eye, mouth, hair and clothing layers.
  Full and LOD1 previews remain 7.09 MiB and 5.48 MiB, a 22.7% reduction. Its
  strict score is 56/100 because the jaw-to-cheek silhouette is more feminine
  and readable, while eyelid thickness, oral topology and secondary facial
  anatomy still block review. Two earlier sub-millimetre v36 parameter passes
  were rejected as visually negligible.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v37.blend`: rebuilds both lower tear menisci as
  tapered 94-vertex rims and adds separate 15-vertex lacrimal-caruncle layers
  at the medial canthi. The former bright triangular gaps beneath both irises
  are absent in the close render, and the upper eyelid fold is tuned to read as
  a crease shadow. Exact checks at neutral and posed frames report zero
  intersections between the rebuilt layers, body, corneas and sclerae. The
  other 38 baseline mesh objects keep identical geometry. Full and LOD1 GLBs
  are 7.08 MiB and 5.47 MiB, a 22.7% reduction, and contain all four explicit
  eye-margin layers, 21 bones and one animation. Its strict score is 57/100;
  integrated eyelid thickness and expression-compatible canthus topology still
  block review.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v38.blend`: replaces the four legacy eyelash layers,
  whose bounds implied 28-33 mm spikes, with tapered forward-curled fibers. Each
  eye uses 26 upper and 12 lower lashes with measured 1.6-8.3 mm lengths,
  inner-to-outer length variation, irregular roots and paired micro-clumping.
  The remaining 38 baseline mesh objects keep identical geometry. Neutral and
  posed audits report zero intersections with the body, sclerae, corneas or
  opposing lash layer. Full and LOD1 GLBs are 7.08 MiB and 5.47 MiB, a 22.7%
  reduction, and preserve all four lash meshes, 21 bones and one animation. Its
  strict score is 58/100. The first evenly spaced v38 pass was rejected because
  it still read as comb teeth.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v39.blend`: replaces both legacy eyebrow meshes,
  whose bounds implied roughly 35 mm comb-like strands, with 120 tapered
  3.0-9.7 mm fibers per side. Inner fibers rise, middle fibers follow the arch
  and tail fibers flatten outward. Each brow also has a separate 175-vertex
  root-density layer with an exportable 0.025-0.425 alpha fade. Neutral and
  posed checks report zero intersections between fibers, density surfaces and
  the body; 40 other baseline meshes keep identical geometry. Full and LOD1
  GLBs are 7.06 MiB and 5.45 MiB, a 22.8% reduction, and preserve both fiber
  groups and both density layers. Its strict score is 59/100. The fiber-only
  first pass was rejected as too sparse.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v40.blend`: replaces the exposed crescent lip
  sheets with continuous 195-vertex upper and lower vermilion surfaces, rebuilds
  the wet margin, and adds separate engine-owned oral-cavity, upper-teeth and
  tongue layers. Neutral and posed BVH checks report zero intersections for all
  audited mouth/body and wet-margin/lip pairs; 41 other v39 meshes retain exact
  geometry. Full and LOD1 GLBs are 7.07 MiB and 5.46 MiB, a 22.7% reduction,
  and expose all six mouth layers, 21 bones and one animation. Its strict score
  is 60/100. Vertex-alpha opening, visible tooth spikes and closed rear lip caps
  were rejected; expression-compatible lip loops and a deformable opening are
  still required before teeth and tongue can appear during speech.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v41.blend`: adds a two-level hierarchy over the
  existing fiber groom: 204 large textured clumps establish the side part and
  crown mass, while 215 offset medium clumps break up broad bands. Rear and
  side cards are clipped to a controlled short-hair hem, ear-crossing sources
  are omitted, and 29 remaining interlayer crossing faces are removed during
  the deterministic build. Neutral and posed BVH checks report zero card/body,
  card/scalp and large/medium intersections; 46 other v40 meshes keep exact
  geometry. Full and LOD1 GLBs are 7.86 MiB and 6.13 MiB, 22.0% smaller, with
  both UV-textured layers, 21 bones and one animation. Its strict score is
  61/100; ear-side grouping, rear hem design, clump rhythm and hair shading
  remain below review quality.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v42.blend`: removes 309 main-groom fibers, six
  guide fibers and three flyaways whose rear or ear-side tips crossed below the
  authored 1.585 m short-hair hem. The card material uses higher roughness and
  lower specular strength to suppress broad grey plastic highlights. Neutral
  and posed checks retain zero intersections across the modified fiber layers,
  body, scalp and both card layers; 45 other v41 meshes preserve exact geometry.
  Full and LOD1 GLBs are 7.58 MiB and 5.75 MiB. LOD1 now drops the medium card
  layer and is 24.1% smaller; the first 19.1% LOD candidate was rejected. Its
  strict score is 62/100 because the rear hem and material response improve,
  while crown breakup, ear-side grouping and dual-lobe hair shading remain open.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v43.blend`: removes 259 main fibers, 31 guides,
  eight flyaways, 41 large cards and 38 medium cards that enter a 10 mm
  exclusion band above the curved front hairline. It replaces the uniform
  transition with 89 shorter, clustered and directionally varied baby hairs.
  Neutral and posed checks report zero intersections across every modified hair
  layer, the body, scalp and card hierarchy; 42 other v42 meshes retain exact
  geometry. Full and LOD1 GLBs are 7.18 MiB and 5.68 MiB, with LOD1 20.9%
  smaller. Its strict score remains 62/100: the front is materially cleaner,
  but temple grouping, the nape edge, crown breakup and engine hair shading
  still fail the production-quality gate.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v44.blend`: replaces the old wide temple and nape
  ribbons with 46 temple, 38 nape and 30 boundary transition clumps that are
  short, tapered, irregularly spaced and projected outside the animated body.
  It also removes 175 ear/nape fibers and 69 card components whose intermediate
  points cross the new silhouette. Neutral and posed checks report zero
  intersections across all modified hair layers, the body, scalp and transition
  hierarchy; 40 other v43 meshes retain exact geometry. Full and LOD1 GLBs are
  6.89 MiB and 5.45 MiB, with LOD1 20.8% smaller. Its strict score is 63/100:
  side and rear grooming earn one point, while crown breakup, card rhythm and
  engine hair shading remain below review quality.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v45.blend`: conforms 133 large and 161 medium hair
  cards to separate 1.10 mm and 2.65 mm scalp shells, narrows every card by a
  deterministic per-clump factor, and replaces the mostly solid card texture
  with an Aevrynd-owned 23-fiber low-background-alpha map. The raised crown
  loops and broad plastic strips disappear without changing 46 other v44 mesh
  objects. Neutral and posed checks report zero card/body, card/scalp and
  inter-card intersections. Full and LOD1 GLBs are 6.90 MiB and 5.46 MiB,

- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v46.blend`: replaces the coarse 72 by 15 undercut

  all four edges. The visible rectangular patch behind the right ear is removed

  intact. Neutral and posed checks report zero intersections with the body,
  scalp or ten other hair layers; 47 other v45 meshes preserve exact geometry.
  Full and LOD1 GLBs are 6.99 MiB and 5.46 MiB, with LOD1 21.9% smaller. Its
  strict score is 65/100; crown flow and engine dual-lobe shading remain open.
- `aevrynd-female-authoring-v47.blend`: bakes the body at Multires level one,
  removes 44 mouth-region faces to create a real opening, and replaces the
  three-row lip sheets with skinned five-row upper and lower lip volumes. Body
  weights are explicitly limited and normalized to four influences; the lips

  posed audits report zero intersections for the complete mouth stack, both
  garments, all eleven hair layers and the undercut hierarchy. Full and LOD1
  GLBs are 7.65 MiB and 5.99 MiB, with LOD1 21.7% smaller; it removes only
  distance-invisible eye and brow micro layers. Its strict score remains 65/100
  because the lip silhouette is still procedural and continuous expression
  loops, eyelid topology and a phoneme pose suite remain incomplete.

Each stage has a neighboring score file. `AUTHORING` stages remain internal and
must not replace the accepted site preview until the repository quality gate is
satisfied.
